# Code of conduct

Wireglyph contributors and users deserve a focused, respectful environment.

Be constructive, assume good intent, criticize ideas rather than people, and
respect privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual
attention, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.

For routine disagreements, pause and return to evidence and the documented
product contract. For serious or sensitive conduct concerns, use the private
reporting channel described in `SECURITY.md`, prefix the report title with
`Conduct:`, and do not open a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
